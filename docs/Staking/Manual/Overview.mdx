The _Manual Installation_ guide aims to reduce your reliance on scripts to make the learning process more straightforward and understandable. The guide provides a detailed overview of the steps involved, ensuring stakers have a clear understanding of the process.

:::info
As of May 21 2025, this **Version 2 Guide** written for **Pectra**🦒 **Hoodi Testnet** :grin:
:::

Setting up a node manually is great for advanced users comfortable with the CLI terminal who want full control and require customization of EL/CL combinations and their configurations. The _Manual Installation_ guide helps you deploy a systemd installation with any EL/CL combination you choose.

:::important
:sparkles: Always test and practice on testnet first.
:::

After installation, use [EthPillar](/EthPillar) to simplify maintenance of your node.
