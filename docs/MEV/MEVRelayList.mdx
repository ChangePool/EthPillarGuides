## Definitions

* **Non-censoring:** Will not filter or censor transactions according to OFAC sanction lists. Also commonly referred to as neutral, unregulated or censorship-free. All types of MEV are permitted.

:::info
Be sure to select the right network. Relays are available for **Mainnet, Hoodi and Sepolia Testnets**.
:::
